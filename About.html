About Me

I strive to allow my clients to be vulnerable while feeling safe when sharing their lived experiences and expressing deep concern about their lives.

  • Independent certified psychologist.
  • Registered practitioner with the Health Professions Council of South Africa.
  • Extensive experience working with teenagers, children, and adults.
  • In-depth knowledge and experience in assessing learners with learning disabilities.
  • Utilises counselling approaches that appreciate and build on client resilience.
  • Believes that most clients hold the answers to their mental health and wellness challenges.
  • Creates an environment that empowers clients to craft their own solutions, enabling possibilities for healing.
  • Normalises feelings to help clients alter perspectives, reduce dependence on the therapist, and strengthen client agency.
  • I offer pro bono therapy online – at the Counselling Hub in Woodstock, Cape Town.

Frequently Asked Questions

  • 1. What kind of challenges can you help with? I provide support for anxiety, depression, trauma, life transitions, relationship struggles, and personal or professional growth. Whether you're feeling stuck, overwhelmed, or seeking clarity, I’m here to help.
  • 2. Do you work with children and teens? Yes! I offer specialized therapy for children, teens, and young adults, including play therapy and developmental assessments to support emotional well-being.
  • 3. What can I expect in my first session? Your first session is a safe and open conversation where we explore your concerns, goals, and how I can best support you. It’s a judgment-free space where you can talk freely.
  • 4. How long does therapy take? Each person’s journey is unique. Some clients see progress in just a few sessions, while others benefit from longer-term support. We’ll work together to find the right approach for you.
  • 5. Do you offer virtual sessions? Yes! I provide both in-person and online sessions, so you can choose what’s most convenient and comfortable for you.
  • 6. What’s the difference between therapy and coaching? Therapy focuses on healing past wounds and emotional struggles, while coaching is about setting goals and creating a fulfilling future. I offer both, depending on your needs.
  • 7. How do I schedule a session? You can reach out via phone, email, or my website to book an appointment. I’ll guide you through the process and answer any questions.
  • 8. Is everything I share confidential? Absolutely. Your privacy is my priority. The only exceptions are cases where safety (yours or others) may be at risk.
