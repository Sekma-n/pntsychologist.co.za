Our Work

Penelope N Tyawa has served in prestigious roles, including Acting Director General and CEO of South Africa's Parliament. Her deep understanding of human behavior, coupled with her strategic leadership skills, allows her to guide clients toward meaningful transformation. She has also made a lasting impact through pro bono counseling and community initiatives.

Specialized Psychological Assessments

Educational Assessments: School readiness, learning disabilities, and career guidance for students.
Developmental Assessments: Holistic evaluations to identify strengths and challenges.
Play Therapy: A child-centered approach to support emotional development and healing.

Counseling & Psychotherapy

Individual Therapy: Focused sessions for adults, youth, and children dealing with anxiety, depression, trauma, or life transitions.
Family & Relationship Counseling: Rebuilding connections and fostering healthy relationships.
Trauma Counseling: Support for individuals recovering from emotional or psychological trauma.

Coaching & Mentoring

Leadership Coaching: Empowering professionals and executives to excel in their roles.
Personal Growth Mentoring: Customized programs to build confidence and resilience.
Women Empowerment Programs: Helping women unlock their full potential and lead with purpose.

Workshops & Training

Mental Wellness Workshops: Tailored programs for schools, organizations, and community groups.
Specialized Training: Brain Working Recursive Therapy (BWRT) and other cutting-edge techniques.
Trauma-Informed Care: Equipping professionals to support individuals in crisis.
