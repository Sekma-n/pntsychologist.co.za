Penelope N Tyawa brings a wealth of experience, combining over 30 years in psychology, leadership, and public service. Her qualifications include degrees in psychology and leadership from renowned institutions such as the University of the Witwatersrand.

Meet Penelope N Tyawa

Penelope N Tyawa has served in prestigious roles, including Acting Director General and CEO of South Africa’s Parliament.

Her deep understanding of human behavior, coupled with her strategic leadership skills, allows her to guide clients toward meaningful transformation. She has also made a lasting impact through pro bono counseling and community initiatives.

At PNT Psychologist, we specialize in providing personalized mental health care and guidance to help individuals, families, and communities thrive. With a foundation built on decades of professional expertise, compassion, and dedication, our mission is to inspire resilience and emotional well-being in everyone we serve.

Penelope N Tyawa, a highly experienced consulting psychologist and strategic leader, our practice offers a safe, nurturing space for individuals to overcome challenges, unlock their potential, and live fulfilling lives.
