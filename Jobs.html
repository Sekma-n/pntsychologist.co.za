Top Specialties

Behavioral Issues: This specialty focuses on understanding and modifying problematic behaviors through techniques like behavioral therapy and cognitive-behavioral therapy. It addresses issues such as impulse control, aggression, and social skills deficits, utilizing strategies to promote positive behavior change.

Depression: Specialists in this area work with individuals experiencing various forms of depression, employing evidence-based therapies such as cognitive behavioral therapy (CBT) and interpersonal therapy to alleviate symptoms and improve emotional well-being. They may also address co-occurring issues like anxiety and low self-esteem.

Self-Esteem: This specialty involves helping clients build a positive self-image and confidence through therapeutic techniques that challenge negative beliefs and promote self-acceptance. Therapists may use cognitive restructuring and skills training to empower individuals to enhance their self-worth.

Expertise

PNT Psychologist is dedicated to providing comprehensive and compassionate care tailored to a wide spectrum of mental health and developmental needs. Our experienced team, Penelope N Tyawa, utilizes evidence-based approaches to address the following areas:

ADHD: We offer thorough assessment and personalized intervention strategies to help individuals manage attention, hyperactivity, and impulsivity, fostering success in academic, professional, and personal spheres.

Anger Management: Our therapists provide guidance and support in developing healthy coping mechanisms for anger, promoting constructive communication and conflict resolution skills.

Antisocial Personality: We offer specialized therapeutic interventions aimed at improving social functioning, empathy, and responsible decision-making, within a structured and supportive environment.

Anxiety: Our practice provides a range of therapeutic techniques, including cognitive-behavioral therapy (CBT), to alleviate anxiety symptoms and equip individuals with effective stress management tools.

Bereavement: We offer compassionate support and counseling to navigate the grieving process, facilitating healing and adjustment after loss.

Career Guidance: Our career guidance services help individuals identify their strengths, interests, and values, providing direction and support in making informed career choices.

Child Psychology: We specialize in addressing the unique emotional, behavioral, and developmental needs of children, utilizing play therapy and other child-centered approaches to promote well-being.

Coping Skills: We empower individuals with practical coping strategies to manage stress, navigate challenges, and enhance resilience in the face of life's difficulties.

Developmental Disorders: Our team conducts comprehensive assessments and develops individualized intervention plans to support individuals with developmental disorders, maximizing their potential and quality of life.

Domestic Abuse/Domestic Violence: We offer a safe and supportive environment for survivors of domestic abuse, providing counseling, advocacy, and resources to promote healing and empowerment.

Education and Learning Disabilities: We provide educational assessments to identify learning disabilities and offer tailored interventions to support academic success and build confidence.

Group Therapy: Our group therapy sessions provide a supportive and collaborative environment for individuals to share experiences, learn from others, and develop interpersonal skills.

Impulse Control Disorders: We offer therapeutic interventions to help individuals manage impulsive behaviors, develop self-control, and improve decision-making skills.

Life Coaching: Our life coaching services empower individuals to set meaningful goals, overcome obstacles, and achieve personal growth and fulfillment.

Oppositional Defiance (ODD): We provide specialized interventions to help children and adolescents with ODD develop positive behavior management techniques and improve family relationships.

Parenting: Our parenting programs offer guidance and support to parents, equipping them with effective strategies to promote healthy child development and strengthen family bonds.

School Issues: We collaborate with students, families, and schools to address academic, social, and emotional challenges, fostering a positive and supportive learning environment.

Stress: Our practice offers stress management techniques and counseling to help individuals reduce stress levels, improve coping skills, and enhance overall well-being.

Thinking Disorders: We provide comprehensive assessment and treatment for individuals with thinking disorders, promoting cognitive clarity, stability, and improved quality of life.

Trauma and PTSD: Our therapists are trained in trauma-informed care and offer evidence-based therapies, such as Trauma-Focused Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (TF-CBT), to help individuals heal from trauma and PTSD.
